    <h1 style="margin-bottom: 0.5em;">Urszula Wiszniowska Ayache, PhD</h1>

    <p><strong>Associate Professor of Strategy</strong><br>
    Oslo Business School, OsloMet</p>

    <p>
      I am a strategy scholar studying how firms create value, coordinate, and adapt under technological and institutional change. My research focuses on platform and ecosystem strategy, business model transformation, and how organizations construct and revise strategic commitments as expectations about the future shift.
    </p>

    <p>
      I am particularly interested in ecosystem and futures thinking: how interdependent organizations coordinate around emerging technologies and societal transitions, how anticipated futures become embedded in strategies, narratives, and relationships, and how firms and ecosystems respond when those futures become uncertain.
    </p>

    <p>
      Drawing on a background in linguistics and computing science, as well as nearly a decade of experience in energy and technology, I combine qualitative research with computational text analysis. My current work also examines technological expectations, AI and digital technologies, and sustainability transitions.
    </p>
